App Name / 应用名称
- 中文：每事每刻
- English: Moments Keeper

----------------------------------------
1. Promotional Text / 推广文本
----------------------------------------
- 中文：用「每事每刻」为你的每件物品记录专属故事和生活瞬间。
- English: Use “Moments Keeper” to capture every story and precious moment of your belongings.

----------------------------------------
2. Description / 应用描述
----------------------------------------
- 中文：
「每事每刻」是一款专注于“物品 + 瞬间”的轻量记录应用。无论是孩子最爱的玩具、一台陪你多年工作的电脑，还是承载回忆的小物件，你都可以为每一件物品创建独立档案，配上照片、分组与笔记，再用“瞬间”记录它在生活中的点滴时刻。

主要功能：
• 管理物品：为每件物品添加名称、生日、照片、分组和备注，建立专属档案。  
• 记录瞬间：用文字、照片、视频或 Live Photo 记录物品相关的生活片段。  
• 时间轴浏览：按时间回顾所有瞬间，看看这些物品陪你走过的每一个阶段。  
• 分组与筛选：支持分组、筛选和搜索，快速找到某件物品或相关瞬间。  
• 本地存储：数据存储在本地设备，配合系统备份使用，更安心、更可控。

如果你常常觉得“好像忘了给这个东西留点什么纪念”，那就让「每事每刻」帮你，把这些不会再重复的日常瞬间留下来。

- English:
“Moments Keeper” is a lightweight journaling app that focuses on the relationship between your belongings and the moments in your life. Whether it’s your child’s favorite toy, the laptop that supports your work, or a small object full of memories, you can create a dedicated profile for each item, add photos, groups, and notes, then record every moment it appears in your daily life.

Key features:
• Manage items: Create a profile for every item with name, birthday, photos, groups, and notes.  
• Record moments: Capture life moments with text, photos, videos, or Live Photos linked to your items.  
• Timeline view: Review all moments in chronological order and see how your items accompany your life.  
• Groups and filters: Use groups, filters, and search to quickly find any item or related moment.  
• Local storage: Data is stored locally on your device and can be protected by system backups for better control and privacy.

If you often think “I wish I had saved something about this object”, let “Moments Keeper” help you preserve those everyday moments that will never happen in the exact same way again.
